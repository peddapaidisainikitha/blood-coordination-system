About EBDCS

We built this platform with one mission: to ensure that no life is lost due to unavailability of blood.

Our Mission

Connecting Lives Through Technology

Blood emergencies happen every day — during surgeries, accidents, and childbirth. The gap between when blood is needed and when it is found can be fatal. EBDCS bridges that gap by bringing together patients, donors, and blood banks on a single, real-time platform.

Our system uses location-based matching, instant notifications, and verified donor networks to dramatically reduce blood search time during emergencies.

Donor Eligibility

Who Can Donate Blood?

Blood donation is safe and simple. Here are the general guidelines.

✅ You CAN Donate If

  • ✅ You are between 18 and 65 years old
  • ✅ Your weight is at least 45 kg
  • ✅ You are in good general health
  • ✅ At least 56 days since your last whole blood donation
  • ✅ Hemoglobin level is within normal range
  • ✅ Blood pressure is within acceptable range

❌ You Should NOT Donate If

  • ❌ You have donated blood within the last 56 days
  • ❌ You have had a tattoo or piercing in the last 6 months
  • ❌ You are currently on certain medications
  • ❌ You have been ill recently (fever, cold, infection)
  • ❌ You are pregnant or recently delivered
  • ❌ You have certain chronic conditions (consult a doctor)
⚠️ Important: Final eligibility is always determined by trained medical staff at the donation centre. This list is for general guidance only.
