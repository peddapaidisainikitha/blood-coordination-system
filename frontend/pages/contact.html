FAQs

How long does it take to find a donor?

In urban areas with registered donors, responses typically arrive within 15–60 minutes after submitting an emergency request.

Is my contact information safe?

Yes. Donor phone numbers are only shared with patients after a request is accepted. All data is stored securely.

How do I register my blood bank?

Email our team using either address listed above. An admin will verify and register your blood bank in the system.
