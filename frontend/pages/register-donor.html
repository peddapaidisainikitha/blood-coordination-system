🩺 Basic Eligibility Criteria

  • Age: 18 – 65 years
  • Weight: at least 45 kg
  • General good health on donation day
  • At least 56 days since last whole blood donation
  • No recent tattooing, piercing, or major surgery

* Final eligibility is determined by medical staff at the donation centre. Registration does not guarantee eligibility.
